03 Our Menu

Everything we make,
all in one place.

Every item below is made at our shop and sold from our counter the same day. Hot snacks through the whole day, Suko Nasto weighed off the shelf, and cold lassi and buttermilk. Type the name of what you want, or just tap a category below.

Hot Snacks

  • Khaman (250 gms). Soft, spongy and light. Steamed besan with mustard seeds, curry leaves and green chilli.
  • White Dhokla (250 gms). Soft white dhokla made from rice and dal. Light on the stomach, with black pepper and coriander.
  • Mini Punjabi Samosa (250 gms). Small crispy samosas filled with spiced potato and green peas.
  • Sev Khamni (250 gms). Crumbled khaman mixed with garlic, green chilli and masala. Topped with pomegranate and sev.
  • Khandvi (250 gms). Soft besan rolls with a light buttermilk tang, topped with coconut and mustard seeds.
  • Coconut Patties (250 gms). Golden potato patties stuffed with sweet coconut, raisins and herbs.
  • Khaman & Sev (250 gms). Our soft khaman with a thick layer of crunchy nylon sev on top.
  • Chinese Samosa (250 gms). Crispy samosas filled with spicy noodles and veggies. Kids love these.
  • Sabudana Vada (Farali) (Plate). Crispy sabudana and potato vada with peanuts and cumin. Comes with green chutney.
  • Farali Peties (Upvas) (250 gms). Upvas special. Potato balls stuffed with coconut, cashew and raisins.

Suko Nasto (dry farsan)

  • Jada Gathiya (250 gms). Thick, crunchy besan gathiya. Best with hot tea or pickle.
  • Bhavnagri Gathiya (250 gms). Light, soft gathiya in Bhavnagar style. Mild, not spicy.
  • Tikha Gathiya (250 gms). Crispy gathiya with red chilli and masala. For those who like it spicy.
  • Jadi Sev (250 gms). Thick sev with black pepper and ajwain.
  • Nylon Sev (100 gms). Very fine, crispy golden sev. Eat as is or sprinkle on anything.
  • Wheat Chakri (250 gms). Crunchy wheat chakri with sesame and spices.
  • Dry Kachori (250 gms). Small flaky kachoris filled with sweet-spicy dal and dry fruit.
  • Mini Bhakharwadi (500 gms). Small bite-size bhakharwadi with sweet-spicy coconut and sesame masala.
  • Big Bhakharwadi (500 gms). Big, crispy bhakharwadi rolls with roasted sweet-spicy masala inside.
  • Sing Bhujiya (250 gms). Crunchy masala-coated peanuts. Perfect with a glass of lassi.
  • Makai Chewda (250 gms). Sweet and salty cornflake mix with peanuts, cashew, raisins and curry leaves.

Lassi & Chhas

  • Punjabi Lassi (200 ml). Thick, chilled sweet lassi with a layer of fresh malai on top.
  • Mango Lassi (200 ml). Thick sweet lassi blended with Alphonso mango.
  • Masala Chhas (Buttermilk) (650 ml). Light masala buttermilk with green chilli, ginger, cumin and coriander.

07 About Us

Same recipe,
same taste, since 1999.

Raghuvanshi Khaman House & Lassi Centre started in 1999, and we have been at it every day since: morning office orders, festival trays, and families who send their guests straight to us. Everything was made in our own kitchen then, and everything is still made in our own kitchen today. The shop has changed a lot. The taste has not changed at all.

1999 · How it started

One counter, one steamer

We started with one steamer and a family recipe. People came once, liked it, and kept coming back every week.

The years after

Silvassa made it a habit

Morning breakfast, evening tea-time packets, wedding and function orders, and two generations of families who tell their guests to get it only from Raghuvanshi.

2026

Moving to a bigger shop

A few doors down at 18 & 19, Perin Complex, with full AC, new counters, a bigger kitchen, and separate shelves for fresh Suko Nasto.

11 October 2026

Grand re-opening

We open the new shop on the first day of Navratri, a good day to start something new. Till then the kitchen is running and our counter at 13, Perin Complex is open as usual.
